About Competitors:

What advantages do competitors have?

Competitors have the advantage of already being established and known by recruiters and companies that are hiring, making you more likely to be seen when using a competing portfolio site.

What disadvantages do competitors have?

Competing portfolio websites have the disadvantage of being inflexible for job seekers who post their resumes on their site.

What techniques do competitors use?

Competitors typically tend to use basic templates and job matching to give recruiters potential matches for their company.

Why take this route instead?

While it is true that using a competitor's website over mine may make my portfolio more likey to be seen, I would also look no different from the many other job seekers that appear to recruiters.  By making my own site to advertise my expertise and qualifications, I can truly separate myself from the many others looking to get a job in the same position as me.
